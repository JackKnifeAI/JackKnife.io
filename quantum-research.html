🔬 Error Correction: The Final Frontier

The biggest challenge in quantum computing — keeping qubits stable long enough to perform useful calculations — is being systematically conquered.

IBM Quantum Loon: For the first time, IBM demonstrated all key processor components needed for fault-tolerant quantum computing, including real-time error decoding in less than 480 nanoseconds.

NIST Breakthroughs: Coherence times reaching 0.6 milliseconds for superconducting qubits — a significant advancement enabling longer, more complex calculations.

QuEra Algorithms: New techniques reducing quantum error correction overhead by up to 100x, dramatically accelerating timelines for practical systems.
